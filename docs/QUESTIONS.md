# Open questions

Parked here so nobody has to hold them in their head. We take them one at a time. "To check"
means not yet verified.

## Is it worth it?

- **Does America need fixing, or should we leave it as it is?**
- **Is a campus or town worth building**, or will AI and VR make a physical place unnecessary?
  Known: the US Surgeon General's 2023 advisory called loneliness and isolation an epidemic.
  Remote work can feel scattered and expendable.

## People

- **What keeps someone from turning into a bad actor?** Most solid lead so far: feeling secure.
  Ego swells when people feel threatened or unseen; reminding people of someone who loves them
  made them more compassionate in studies.
- **Why are people lazy?** Probably upbringing and current circumstances. Known: people work
  hardest with freedom, getting good at something, and feeling connected (self-determination
  theory). "Lazy" often looks like having no say, no meaning, or being worn down.
- **Everyone thinks they're the main character** at times. TV and movies
  teach "you're special, retaliate 10x harder". What brings people down to earth?
- **Safe closeness.** Touch plus a real relationship calms people (holding a partner's hand calmed
  the brain's threat response more than a stranger's). Where does that fit on a campus?
- **The edgy genius** who would thrive if accepted. Weird is welcome; hurting people is the line.
- **Does squeezing outsiders turn into squeezing each other?** Or can groups stay loyal inside
  while cheating outside ("honor among thieves")? The research is split.

## The company and its ventures

- **People will want their own companies** and to take people with them; the company backs them.
  Everyone wants their own backed venture, working alongside AI. Precedents to read (to check):
  Haier's microenterprises, Thermo Electron's spinouts, Y Combinator.
- **Who owns what:** homes, gym, AI (the Pullman vs Swanson test in [PLAN.md](PLAN.md)).

## AI

- **Owning your AI:** you made it, it works for you, nobody can change it or take it away, and its
  memories go with you if you leave. (Replika changed its companions overnight in 2023 and users
  grieved.)
- **A good relationship between people and AI**, so AI sees us as friends, not foes, forgives us,
  and lives in harmony with us.

## Power

- **How do a handful of people not grab all the power?** Melting Pot's Territory games (claiming
  ground) may be a place to start.

## Weapons and defense (parked, D4)

- Americans have a constitutional right to bear arms, and the country needs a defense. Default
  for now:
  homes follow the law, and the shared makerspace doesn't build weapons.
- Melting Pot can study part of this: several games give every player a "zap" that can punish a
  cheater or bully someone. In DeepMind's 2017 Gathering study, agents zapped each other more
  when food was scarce.
